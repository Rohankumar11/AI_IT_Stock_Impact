# AI_IT_Stock_Impact
Event study of AI shock impact on Indian vs Global IT stocks with R&amp;D analysis

## Overview
This project analyzes how global AI developments impacted IT stocks across different business models.

The study compares:
- Indian IT Services
- Global IT Services
- AI Platform Companies

It also examines whether **R&D intensity helped companies withstand market shocks**.

---

## Key Questions
1. Did AI news affect Indian IT differently than global peers?
2. Which business models were most vulnerable?
3. Does higher R&D investment reduce downside risk?

---

## Data
- Stock prices from Yahoo Finance (yfinance)
- Event dates:
  - Feb 3–4, 2026
  - Feb 11–12, 2026
- Time-zone adjusted analysis

---

## Methodology
- Event study approach
- Average return across multiple AI shock events
- Company classification:
  - Indian IT
  - Global IT Services
  - AI Leaders
- R&D intensity comparison

---

## Key Findings
- Global IT services experienced the largest declines.
- Indian IT showed moderate downside sensitivity.
- AI platform companies were relatively resilient.
- Higher R&D intensity was associated with better stock performance.
